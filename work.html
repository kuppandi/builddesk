Two things I deliberately did not do: guess a phone number that was too short to fix, and delete rows silently because their name was blank. Bad values get blanked and counted, not invented. Rows that fail get flagged, not thrown away. If your data matters enough to clean, it matters enough that you can see what was changed.

Why you're looking at a CSV and not a screenshot
